A program that writes a one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "Midnight Fridge Roast" bounty.

The roast:

> My smart fridge locks at midnight, as if a slice of leftover cold pizza at 1 a.m. is a national emergency it alone must prevent.

The fridge's petty midnight rule is the whole joke: a slice of cold pizza escalates into a national emergency only the fridge can prevent. Picked by a head-to-head contest of 6 candidates, judged on the bounty brief's own order.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
